\textit{Note.} Cumulative-logit ordinal model (\texttt{model\_4\_ord}) refitted with the coefficient prior changed from $\mathcal{N}(0,\,1)$ to $\mathcal{N}(0,\,0.5)$; data, formula, cut-point priors and sampler settings identical (4 chains $\times$ 1000 draws after 1000 tuning steps, seed 42). Coefficients are on the logit scale; the continuous predictors are $z$-scored, so each is the change in log-odds of a higher calling category per 1\,SD. HDI = highest-density interval. $\Delta$ = posterior mean under the tighter prior minus that under the reference prior; $\Delta/\mathrm{SD}$ expresses that shift in reference posterior SDs ($\lvert\Delta/\mathrm{SD}\rvert<0.1$ = negligible relative to posterior uncertainty). An HDI width ratio $<1$ means the tighter prior narrows the interval. Inference counts as unchanged when the sign of the posterior mean and the exclusion or inclusion of zero by the 95\% HDI both agree across priors.
